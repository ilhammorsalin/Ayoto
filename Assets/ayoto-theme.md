## Aesthetic Categorization

Based on product naming conventions and core materials, Ayoto's catalog sits at the intersection of **Contemporary Italian Luxe** and **Organic Modernism**. It caters to buyers seeking polished, high-end, statement-driven interiors rather than raw, rustic, or hyper-minimalist spaces.

*   **Primary Style: Contemporary Italian Luxe (Euro-Contemporary)**
    *   *Evidence:* The heavy reliance on highly polished, dramatic materials like High-Gloss UV Marble, Black Gold Natural Marble, and lacquered finishes. Naming conventions like *Firenze, Palermo,* and *Sereno* directly evoke high-end Italian design houses.
*   **Secondary Style: Organic Modernism (Biophilic Design)**
    *   *Evidence:* Product names like *Stingray, Whale, Vine,* and *Plateau* indicate that the furniture features fluid, curved, or sculptural silhouettes inspired by nature, counterbalancing the hard, glossy materials.

*(Note: The brand firmly distances itself from Japanese Minimalist, Japandi, or Scandinavian aesthetics. Those styles rely on raw, matte materials and strict functional lightness, whereas Ayoto favors engineered, glamorous, and polished visual weight.)*

## Product Nomenclature & Materials

The product catalog reveals two distinct naming conventions alongside a focus on high-contrast surface materials.

*   **Italian & Architectural Lines:** Products named *Firenze, Palermo, Modo, Elysia, Sereno, Altura*.
*   **Organic & Sculptural Lines:** Products named *Stingray, Whale, Dwarf Whale, Vine, Plateau, Cheetah*.
*   **Core Finishes:** Lacquer-edged High Gloss UV Marble Board.
*   **Premium Surfaces:** Black Gold Natural Marble.
*   **Pricing Tiers:** Ranges from entry-level accents (~৳11,780) to premium centerpiece items (~৳74,215).

## Services & Site Features

Beyond e-commerce, the current site metadata highlights foundational business services aimed at both direct consumers and B2B clients.

*   **Trade Partnership:** A dedicated portal and programs for interior designers and architects.
*   **Design Services:** Complimentary spatial planning and design consultations for entire homes.
*   **Omnichannel Presence:** Active promotion of physical showroom locations alongside the online catalog.
*   **User Features:** Account registration, product comparison tools (up to 4 products), and filtering by price range and stock availability.
