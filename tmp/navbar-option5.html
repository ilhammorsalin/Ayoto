Tab-Based Category Takeover

Inspired by: Net-a-Porter, luxury interiors portals

  • ROW 1 — Logo + primary links (Inspiration, Catalog, Locations, Showcase, About). Design Services as a standout CTA button. Utility icons far-right.
  • ROW 2 — Horizontal tab strip: Best Seller · New Arrival · Classics · By Furniture · By Room · Bedroom · Kitchen · Modular Kitchen. Hides on scroll-down.
  • PANEL — When a tab is clicked, a visual sub-panel slides under showing furniture type cards with icons/thumbnails. Not a dropdown — it's a persistent strip.
  • Modular Kitchen tab opens a special panel with "Book Appointment" CTA in brand color.
  • In production, panel cards would show real product thumbnail images from the catalog.
  • Best for: Immersive, visual browsing. Feels like a catalog itself. Extremely differentiating.
